All people, period times, rooms, loads and covers are invented. School day start and end come from the GIIS handbook (p.22); Grade 7 subjects and language choices from p.52; language hours from p.141–142.

## Steps

1. Use the same Claude Design project as the other modules, so MinV and the earlier screens are already there.
2. Attach the two files.
3. Paste Prompt 1. It builds the coordinator's cover board for today.
4. Add one screen at a time with the follow-ups: class timetable, timetable builder with clashes, teaching groups and assignments, teacher week and load, Farah's "My day" on a phone, bell schedule and rooms.
5. Check each round against the review checklist.

## Limits

- Section 6 of the brief lists what is still open. Show those as "Pending decision".
- Period times, the 5-day week, maximum load and the cover limit are sample campus settings, not group defaults.
- DL-131…DL-145 are all Confirmed (Henry, 24–25 Nov 2026).
