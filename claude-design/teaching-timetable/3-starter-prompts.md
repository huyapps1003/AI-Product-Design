## Review checklist

- [ ] Never shows why a teacher is away.
- [ ] "Publish" is disabled while any Must fix clash exists; only the Principal sees it enabled.
- [ ] Cover suggestions never include someone at the cover limit or teaching in that period.
- [ ] Language blocks appear as one cell with three groups, not three separate lessons.
- [ ] One-off changes are made on today's lesson, never on the Live timetable.
- [ ] Period times, maximum load and cover limit say "sample" or "Pending decision"; OWIS says "Not set".
- [ ] No fee status anywhere.
