- [ ] Colleagues and coordinators only ever see "Away", never the leave type or reason.
- [ ] No salary, bank or tax field anywhere.
- [ ] Tier 3 fields show a lock with who can see them.
- [ ] Not cleared never reads as suspended or dismissed; it says what is blocked.
- [ ] Interview feedback from others is hidden until you submit yours.
- [ ] Entitlements, checks and thresholds say "sample" or "Pending decision"; OWIS says "Not set".
- [ ] Appraisal ratings never appear outside the person, reviewer, Principal and HR.
