All people, entitlements, checks, vacancies and ratings are invented. The Principal as Designated Person for Child Protection comes from the GIIS handbook (p.210–213); IB training at workshops from p.138. Everything else is a sample setting.

## Steps

1. Use the same Claude Design project as the other modules, so MinV and the earlier screens are already there.
2. Attach the two files.
3. Paste Prompt 1. It builds HR's staff directory with compliance chips.
4. Add one screen at a time with the follow-ups: staff profile, leave approvals and team calendar, apply for leave on a phone, compliance dashboard, recruitment board, joining and leaving checklists, appraisal.
5. Check each round against the review checklist.

## Limits

- Section 6 of the brief lists what is still open. Show those as "Pending decision".
- Leave entitlements, required checks and the 3-day Principal threshold are sample GIIS SG settings, not group defaults; OWIS shows "Not set".
- DL-152…DL-164 are Assumed until Henry reviews them one by one.
